Issue #5 · design mock · a drawing, never shipped

Loud only when it matters.

Blunt, nocturnal, quiet when there is nothing to do. Every surface leads with a verdict. Every state that needs you sits on a solid plate; the one state that doesn't is the quietest thing on the screen.

Not this: green ticks and pastel status chips, a parking map, emoji, "Heads up!" before the what. No colour carries meaning: the Lock Screen widget has none.

The side choice

Two taps: press and hold, then a side. Scriptable comes forward with a system alert, the spot sheet. If nobody taps, the earlier side's reminder stands unchanged.

When the city gives no side name (blockside is null on 255 whole blocks), the side is worked out from the block's centreline. That agrees with the city's own names on 99.98% of rows. The buttons then read "South-east side (from map) · Berry St" and "North-west side (from map) · Berry St". If the map and the city disagree, there are no buttons and the earlier reminder stands.

Light and dark appearance

The notification plate belongs to iOS and follows the phone's appearance. The words carry the difference between the heads-up and the can't-tell state in both.
